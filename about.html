About Fresh Cut

Our story, mission, and the team behind your perfect lawn

Our Story

Founded in 2010 by landscape architect John Peterson, Fresh Cut Lawn Care began as a small operation serving residential clients in Calgary.

What started as a one-man operation has grown into Western Canada's premier lawn care service provider, with teams in six cities across three provinces.

We remain committed to our founding principles: exceptional service, environmental responsibility, and creating beautiful outdoor spaces.

Our Mission & Values
