Frequently Asked Questions

Most lawns benefit from 4-5 feedings per year, timed with the natural growth cycles of your grass. We recommend a spring feeding, two summer feedings, and a fall feeding for optimal results.

Early morning (between 4am and 10am) is ideal because it allows the water to soak into the soil before the heat of the day causes evaporation. Evening watering can promote fungal growth.

Never remove more than 1/3 of the grass blade at one time. For most cool-season grasses, maintain a height of 2.5-3.5 inches. Taller grass shades the soil, reducing water evaporation and weed growth.
